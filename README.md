Living in Malaysia's warm, humid climate can create favourable conditions for common household pests such as ants, cockroaches, termites, rats and bed bugs. Understanding how these pests enter your home and what attracts them can help you maintain a cleaner and more comfortable living environment.

**Practical Ways to Reduce Household Pest Problems**

1. Keep Your Home Clean
Store food in sealed containers, clean kitchen surfaces regularly and dispose of rubbish properly. Removing food sources can help discourage ants, cockroaches and rodents.
2. Seal Potential Entry Points
Inspect doors, windows, wall gaps and pipe openings for possible pest entry points. Sealing suitable gaps can help reduce pest access to indoor spaces.
3. Reduce Moisture and Standing Water
Repair leaking pipes, keep damp areas well ventilated and remove standing water around your property. These measures can help reduce conditions that attract certain pests, including mosquitoes.
4. Inspect Furniture and Wooden Structures
Check mattresses, bed frames and upholstered furniture for signs of bed bugs. Look for possible termite activity around wooden structures, particularly when you notice unexplained damage.

**When Should You Consider Professional Pest Control?**

If pests continue to appear despite preventive measures, or you notice signs of a significant infestation, professional assessment may be helpful.
